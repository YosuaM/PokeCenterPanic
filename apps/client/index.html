    A QUICK STAFF BRIEFING

    Care. Coordinate. Repeat.

    Work together to treat as many Pokémon as you can during a six-minute shift. Play solo or bring up to three friends.

    1. Welcome your patients. Pick up a patient from the waiting area and check their treatment icons.
    2. Give them the right care. Use the healing machine for injuries. Prepare Burn Heal or Antidote at the medicine table.
    3. Send them home happy. Carry fully treated patients to reception for discharge before their patience runs out.
    4. Play to your strengths. Each Pokémon has a specialty: Chansey heals, Audino mixes medicine, Machoke carries and repairs, Meowth runs. Press Q for Chansey's Soft-Boiled or Audino's Calm.
