# Layered clinic v2 — image prompts

Created using the built-in image generation tool, using the existing original clinic illustration as the visual reference. No third-party binary artwork was added.

The five selected images were exported as PNG. During registration, the landscape was fitted from 1671 × 941 to the common 1672 × 941 canvas. The corrected smoke was translated by (-80, -88) pixels to align its tip with the new chimney. These are export/compositing adjustments, not additional painted content.

## 01-landscape

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image: edit target, existing twilight clinic illustration. Produce ONLY the opaque BACKGROUND LANDSCAPE layer for a registered parallax scene. Preserve exact 1672x941 canvas, framing, mountain ridge placement, lake, distant village and indigo/violet/orange twilight palette, crisp detailed pixel clusters. Remove ALL clouds, smoke, the entire clinic/building/chimney, paths, lamps, fences, flowers, close shrubs and ALL near dark pine trees including edge framing trees and trees behind clinic. Inpaint their former areas as continuous unobstructed distant blue-violet mountains/valley forest and lake, with cloudless starred sky above. Keep original horizon around y=300..400, mountain peak at x=870 y=280; do not enlarge mountains or shift camera. The removed foreground will be restored on separate transparent layers. Fill the WHOLE frame with plausible distant landscape, no blank holes. Do not retain any near silhouettes, roof, chimney or smoke. No text, no labels, no characters. This is a background plate, not a new composition.

## 02-clouds

Keep exact 1672x941 canvas/framing and exact position/scale of the pink lavender pixel-art cloud bands across the upper 360 pixels of the reference. Recover complete cloud forms where pine branches or chimney smoke previously covered them. Retain pink highlights and violet shadows, sharp pixel cluster edges. No rectangular sky patches, no opaque colored background, no checkerboard painted into pixels, no labels, no text. The clouds will be composited using normal source-over onto the original indigo twilight sky and drift horizontally. Keep the lower 60 percent completely transparent; do not center/resize the cloud band, do not add new large clouds.

Remove clinic, chimney, trees, ground and everything else; smoke only, in its stated coordinates, not centered or enlarged. No backdrop, no text, no checkerboard pixels. This is a separate overlay to move upward, expand and fade over the clinic layer.
